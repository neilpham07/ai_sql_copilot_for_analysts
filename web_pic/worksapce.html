SELECT m.name AS merchant_name, SUM(t.amount) AS total_revenue FROM merchants m JOIN transactions t ON m.id = t.merchant_id WHERE t.created_at >= DATE_TRUNC('month', CURRENT_DATE) AND t.status = 'completed' GROUP BY m.id, m.name ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

Logic Breakdown

1 Identify Tables

Referencing merchants and transactions from active schema.

2 Resolve Joins

Linking merchant_id foreign key relation for data integrity.

3 Apply Filters

Filtering by current month and 'completed' transaction status.

4 Aggregate

Calculating SUM of revenue per merchant and sorting descending.
